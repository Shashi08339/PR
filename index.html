    Our Story

    Baked with Passion,
    Served with Love

    Sweet Crumbs began in a tiny kitchen in Bengaluru, where a grandmother's recipes met modern technique. For over 15 years, we've been waking up before dawn so your morning starts perfectly.

    Every loaf, every Eclair, every celebration cake is shaped by hand — never by machine. We source heritage grains, local dairy, and seasonal fruit to keep each bite extraordinary.
